Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 (added in-game reports and voice chat)

Short version: PhysBox VR has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Voice chat is live only and never recorded. To make multiplayer and moderation work, it creates an anonymous player code for your device and shares your in-game name with the people you play with. There are no accounts, emails or passwords, and nothing is sold.

Who we are

PhysBox VR is a free game made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”) in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email jburt4658@gmail.com.

We don't collect your real name, email address, age, location, contacts, payment details or your Meta account information, and we never record voice chat.

Moderation and bans

If a player breaks the rules, we may ban their player code. The ban list holds the player code, the reason for the ban and when it happened. The game checks this list regularly so that banned players can't join online games. The game also keeps a list of recently met players on the moderator's own device, so that rule-breakers can still be identified after they leave.

In-game reports. If you report a player from the Players menu, we store: your player code and name, their player code and name, the reason you picked, the map, where in the map they were standing, and which online game you were in. Only moderators can read reports. We use them to check what happened and decide whether to ban someone. Reports are deleted once they're no longer needed, and at most 12 months after they're closed. You can also report players through Meta's own tools, which are handled under Meta's privacy policy.

Services we use

  • Epic Online Services (Epic Games) runs online lobbies and player-to-player connections. It signs you in anonymously without an Epic account. See Epic's privacy policy.
  • Supabase stores the ban list and moderator list. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Meta Horizon Store distributes the game on Quest. See Meta's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Voice chat

Voice chat only works in online games, and only after you allow microphone access. You can choose Open Mic, Push to Talk or Off in the Multiplayer menu, and mute any player from the Players menu. Voice goes straight to the players in your game (relayed by the host) and isn't recorded, stored or sent to us. If someone abuses voice chat, report them with the "Voice chat abuse" reason. Because voice isn't recorded, the report itself is what we go on.

Stored on your device

The game saves your settings, chosen name, tutorial progress and an identity file on your headset or PC. These files never leave your device unless stated above. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

How long we keep it

Gameplay data is gone when the session ends. A ban record is kept for as long as the ban is active and is deleted when the ban is lifted. Reports are kept for up to 12 months after they are closed. We don't keep any other data about you.

Children

PhysBox VR is intended for the age rating shown on its store page. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the game doesn't ask for names, emails or ages, there is very little to collect. If you think a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete data linked to your player code, or object to how we use it. Email us with your player code, which you can find in the Multiplayer menu, and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub, which hosts it, may log basic technical details such as your IP address for security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
